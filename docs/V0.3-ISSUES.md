# v0.3 issue coverage and evidence limits

This ledger maps the v0.2 reports numbered #23–35 to the v0.3 implementation and regression evidence. It describes the supported cases, rather than claiming that all circuit research or all task acceptance criteria are complete. The tests below use public, deterministic fixtures. They do not establish performance or optimisation quality on the reporter's private 50-million-instruction task.

## Research acceptance that remains open

- **[#9](https://github.com/almond-lab/strata/issues/9), semantic lifting:** exact algebraic and effect recovery does not establish the requested ≥90% typed-Toffoli and ≥95% stripped-boundary benchmark on tagged arithmetic programs. High-level arithmetic-stage coverage remains unestablished.
- **[#10](https://github.com/almond-lab/strata/issues/10), autonomous proposals:** generic constructors, multi-round exploration and reusable checked templates are available and tested on fixtures. The requested top-k historical recovery benchmark on at least three of four real bases has not been supplied. Do not substitute fixture gains for that acceptance criterion.
- **[#19](https://github.com/almond-lab/strata/issues/19), imported attempt history:** the actual 329-row `ATTEMPTS.jsonl` was imported into an isolated temporary store, with unchanged source bytes; the aggregate, hash-bound receipt is [V0.3-HISTORY-IMPORT.json](V0.3-HISTORY-IMPORT.json). All 329 rows remain explicitly unbound because they supply no semantic contract fingerprints or verified binding map. Requested labels include one proof and three builds, but prose labels do not become checked evidence. The separate 36-row archive was also imported and is not counted as additional attempt-ledger evidence. Real multi-hundred-row ingestion is established; actual binding and subsequent base-change apply/reopen acceptance remain open. No contracts or reopening results were invented.

## Reproducing the focused evidence

```sh
.venv/bin/pytest tests/test_integration_v03.py tests/test_runtime_v03.py tests/test_tensor_v03.py tests/test_domains_v03.py tests/test_semantic_effects.py tests/test_streaming_v03.py -q
STRATA_REAL_SANDBOX=1 .venv/bin/pytest tests/test_runtime_v03.py::test_virtualenv_invocation_and_environment_identity tests/test_runtime_v03.py::test_native_binary_bundle_config_ingest_and_replay -q
```

The second command requires working bubblewrap namespaces. There is no production fallback that executes an untrusted verifier outside the sandbox. Transport substitutes used by ordinary unit tests are explicitly confined to test fixtures.
