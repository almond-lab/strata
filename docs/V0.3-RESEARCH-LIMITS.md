# v0.3 evidence and limits

The implementation supports a bounded research loop. It does not establish
automatic understanding of every arithmetic stage, universal restructuring, or
the historical target of proposing at least three of four restructures without
hints. [ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md) records historical gates separately from
software and fixture validation.

| Result | What it establishes | What remains outside its scope |
|---|---|---|
| Exact Boolean/sign window | Supported boundary functions and exact sign exponent, under recorded clean assumptions | Fractional rotations, arbitrary quantum instruments, task intent |
| HMR/conditional evidence | Native outcome-parametric basis trajectories, classical outputs, phase debts and supported repayment | Equivalence of changed measurement instruments or arbitrary-state quantum channels |
| Finite-domain equality | Every complete promised assignment and every aligned formal HMR outcome, with equal physical width, full physical/classical outputs and sign | All-input equality, omitted-wire layouts, unlisted inputs, distributional equivalence, host acceptance |
| Function-of witness | A replayed polynomial relation in current support values, within its searched family | A universal negative if the bounded family finds none |
| Information slack | Exact affine rank or bounded joint distinct-state count | A free clean wire, a cheap encoder, consumer compatibility |
| Compression construction | A complete reversible permutation and inverse, checked on its bounded state space | Native cost for unsynthesized wider tables; automatic physical width reduction |
| Observable phase elimination | Emitted native circuit, restored outputs and phase under explicit clean boundary assumptions | Arbitrary dirty-workspace elimination or higher-degree phase synthesis |
| File resource delta | Actual static emitted width and operation counts under the supplied formula | Conditional mean cost, host `Q_total`, portfolio or official score |
| Learned template | A previously verified gate-pattern transformation with checked predicates and new boundary replay | Learned model weights, universal algebraic discovery, validity at unmatched scales |

## Constructive scope

The finite checker aligns ordered HMR events by measured wire, destination,
activity and value on every checked input/outcome. Equal event counts alone
are insufficient. Unproved alignment returns unknown under this conservative
branch model. It does not rule out a host verifier proving a valid rewrite
that changes measurement provenance or the measurement instrument.

Automatic dependency search tries bounded linear/nonlinear polynomials and
minimal support sets. `discover_compressions` also searches bounded cuts. The
compression API enumerates the **joint** selected support: eleven codes and an
independent tag occupy 22 states, requiring five bits. It does not infer a
four-bit encoding from the code register alone.

`constructive_compression` supplies executable permutation tables and their
inverse up to the chosen width budget. For at most three selected wires it
also synthesizes native X/CX/CCX gates and independently replays them. Both
encoder and decoder costs appear in the bill. Wider tables explicitly report
that native synthesis/cost is unavailable. A lease exists only at the proved
cut and under the recorded domain or clean assumptions.

The phase constructor handles complete restored circuits whose sign exponent
can be emitted with NEG/Z/CZ/CCZ. It verifies every physical output under the
clean promise. Independent concrete replay is exhaustive within its input
budget; a larger case reports that replay as unknown while retaining the
separate symbolic proof. Public physical wire indices are never renumbered.
An omitted trailing clean wire can reduce `Q`; removing an interior clean hole
alone cannot. Retained unused public inputs still count in the layout.

## Restricted lower bounds

Boolean counting and exact-small synthesis use free XOR/NOT/constants and
charged two-input ANDs. Bilinear tensor flattening bounds use a **declared
bipartition**, left-linear times right-linear products, and free XOR fanout.
Quadratic scalar rank bounds aggregate by maximum, not by summing across
outputs that may share products. These are explicit restricted circuit models.

An exact tensor decomposition is a construction upper bound until a proved
lower bound matches it. Budget exhaustion can still return a verified upper
bound without establishing an optimum. Substitution bounds without a
certificate remain unestablished. Reversible lowering must price actual
helpers, adapters, fanout and cleanup; a tensor product count is not itself a
Toffoli, measurement, physical width or host-cost floor.

Finite-domain floors restrict functions to the declared rows and carry their
domain hash. Measurement/classical channels are excluded from Boolean gate
floors. No small-size or finite-promise bound is silently promoted to a broader
contract.

## Measured arithmetic is not priced by Boolean floors

`strata floors` uses a free-XOR/two-input-AND Boolean model. A window containing
HMR or classical input/control channels can have exact supported semantics and
still return `unknown`: that gate model does not price measurement, feed-forward
or phase-debt repayment. This includes typical measured-uncomputation products
and divisions. `--clean-inputs` can establish reset legality; it does not make
those operations disappear from the cost model.

Do not treat measured outputs as free or delete their correction network to
obtain a purported full-component lower bound. A separately certified pure
Boolean subregion may have a scoped bound, with all surrounding work still
priced separately. A measurement-aware full-component floor is not implemented.

Wire-list flags such as `--clean-inputs`, `--support`, and `--internal-bits`
accept comma-separated nonnegative indices and inclusive ranges: `16..27`,
`16-27`, or `0,4..7,12`. Expansion is bounded to one million indices. Malformed,
descending or oversized ranges produce a named usage error.

## Search, scaling and reporting

The registered search currently offers observable phase elimination, direct
consumer synthesis, bounded bilinear decomposition and analysis-directed tensor
residual proposals with native finalist checks. Trusted constructors
and agent-written native candidates can extend it. The agent remains
responsible for originating new hypotheses when these constructors cannot
build one. A no-gain run closes only the actual bounded constructions tested.

All verified states and attempts remain in the run archive; only a bounded
beam advances. Stored Pareto alternatives can be revisited. Learned templates
currently transfer matching native gate patterns modulo wire renaming and
explicit predicates, with fresh verification. They are not broad new
arithmetic identities inferred from a successful example.

Streaming discovery bounds Python windows and keeps repeated occurrences on
disk. The SDK still parses a whole native circuit and spools text before
scanning. Timings and peak RSS describe that concrete run. A requested range
reports its own coverage; a context-cut fragment is not a proved replacement
boundary. Whole-episode construction has a separate operation budget.

Report artifact hashes, exact scope, tested inputs/outcomes, emitted resources,
objective, retained alternatives, and unknown obligations. Host verification,
official runs, high-level recovery coverage, and historical top-k milestones
need their own receipts. Passing generic fixtures does not supply them.

## Numeric-first scope

Numeric candidates, error ledgers, convergence models, shape maps and stage
pipelines are first-class library objects. The project's pinned reference
callback still supplies its scientific arithmetic and block attribution. Strata
does not infer a chemistry Hamiltonian, invent an official metric from an array,
or supply a universal differentiable version of discrete rounding.

Fast scorer calibration compares listed artifacts, acceptance flags, attribution
and final cost under explicit tolerances. It does not prove agreement on unseen
candidates. Gradient checks are bounded finite-difference diagnostics; discrete
word changes use separately priced moves or explicitly labelled surrogates.
Normalization, repair and refit stages are declared plugins whose final cost,
trainable masks and neutrality invariants are recorded. Nothing is assumed free.

Trend probabilities are conditional fitted-model estimates, not independently
verified pass rates. Exact rational quadratic/grid minima certify the supplied
finite model, not the complete nonlinear fit. Candidate promotion still uses
the existing independent full-artifact gates.

Checkpoint jobs provide local synchronous execution, source pinning, atomic
checkpoint records, per-job budgets and locking. They are not a cluster compute
broker, global RAM/disk reservation service, background daemon or distributed
exactly-once scheduler. The source-pinned readiness packager can create a local
verified design; it never submits externally.
